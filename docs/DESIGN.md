## 1. Principles

1. **Color carries meaning, never decoration.** Each pastel has one job (§2). If a new element needs color, it borrows an existing meaning or stays neutral.
2. **The day is a train line.** The itinerary is drawn as a rail line in the city's color, with each item a station. The day badge copies the station-number signs on Japanese rail maps (`KYO 03`). This is the app's signature.
3. **Pictograms, not emoji.** A small custom line-icon set in the style of Japanese public signage (§7). Icons sit next to words to speed up scanning. They never replace a label.
4. **Staff cards break the theme.** Anything a Japanese speaker reads across a counter is paper-white, near-black and huge.
5. **Quiet system states.** Sync, offline, update and staging use neutrals only, so they never compete with city, budget or allergy colors.
6. **Readable first.** Body text is 17 pt and grows with the iPhone's Text Size setting, but never shrinks below the §9 minimums. Primary actions sit low on the screen.

## 4. Type

| Role | Token | Size (default) | Floor | Face |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display (countdown) | `--fs-display` | 2.75rem · 47 | — | Fraunces 500, SOFT 60 |
| Title 1 (screen title) | `--fs-title-1` | 2rem · 34 | — | Fraunces |
| Title 2 (greeting, section) | `--fs-title-2` | 1.5rem · 25.5 | — | Fraunces |
| Title 3 (Now/Next item, hotel and idea names) | `--fs-title-3` | 1.25rem · 21 | — | Fraunces |
| Body | `--fs-body` | 1rem · 17 | 17 | System (SF Pro) |
| Secondary | `--fs-secondary` | 0.9rem · 15.3 | 15 | System |
| Caption (nothing smaller) | `--fs-caption` | 0.8rem · 13.6 | 13 | System |
| Staff-card Japanese, hotel address | `--fs-card-ja` | 2rem · 34 | 32 | Hiragino Sans 700 (address 600) |
| Allergen word | `--fs-card-allergen` | 2.6rem · 44 | 40 | Hiragino Sans 800 |
| Booking reference | `--fs-card-ref` | 2.6rem · 44 | 40 | System 800, tabular |

- **Sizes scale up but never below their floor.** On iOS, `html { font: -apple-system-body }` (gated to iOS only) ties `1rem` to the Text Size setting. Each floored token is `max(rem, px)`, so a larger Text Size grows the text and a smaller one leaves the §9 minimums in place. This settles the conflict in §9 between "respect Text Size" and "nothing under 13 pt".
- **Fraunces** is used only for headings and names, never for numbers or running text. It's OFL-licensed. The app will **self-host** it in `fonts/` (HANDOFF §7). The preview loads it from Google Fonts. Fallback: `ui-serif`, Iowan Old Style, Georgia.
- **Japanese** uses the system Hiragino Sans (`:lang(ja)` or `.ja`). No Japanese web font ships.
- **Numbers** (money, times, distances, booking refs) use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (`.num`).
- Uppercase labels (`.eyebrow`) get 0.08em letter-spacing. Headings use `text-wrap: balance`.

## 5. Space, shape, touch

- Spacing scale (4-based): `--sp-1` 4 · `--sp-2` 8 · `--sp-3` 12 · `--sp-4` 16 · `--sp-5` 24 · `--sp-6` 32 · `--sp-7` 48.
- Radii by role, not one radius everywhere: `--r-chip` 6 (tags, chips) · 10–14 (day badge, banners, pictogram tiles, quick-search tiles) · `--r-card` 16 (cards) · `--r-sheet` 24 (staff cards, sheets) · 999 (buttons, pills).
- Every tappable element is at least **44 × 44 pt** (`--tap`). Two smaller-looking controls get a larger hit area: the allergy badge looks 28 pt tall but taps as 44 pt (`::before` hit area), and the checklist **row** toggles while the 44 pt ring button carries the accessible state (`aria-pressed`). Category chips are labels today. If they become filter buttons in Nearby, they need 44 pt height.
- Side gutter 16 px. Layout works from 375 pt wide with no horizontal scroll (checked Oct 13 in the browser pane at 375 × 812: `scrollWidth` = 375).
- Safe areas: the tab bar pads by `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. The app shell will pad the top by `env(safe-area-inset-top)`. The tab bar is **opaque**, so content scrolling under it can't weaken label contrast.
- Primary actions use the **Moonlight** button (light fill, ink text), so color stays reserved for meaning. Secondary actions use a raised navy button. Quiet actions (Skip) use text only.

## 7. Pictograms

Drawn for Brillo: 24 × 24 inline SVG `<symbol>`s, 1.8 stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`. In the app the sprite lives in `index.html`, so icons work offline with no extra requests. They are decorative (`aria-hidden`) because a text label always sits beside them.

**Color rule:** pictograms take the city color only inline on itinerary stops and Now/Next titles (via `var(--city)`). `.pict-tile` is always neutral (`--text`), even inside a `.city-*` screen, so an idea tile on Today stays neutral. Pictograms never use a reserved meaning color, except the Sushi quick search's amber edge, which is the Caution meaning.

## 8. Motion

Minimal. No entrance animations. The only planned motion is the cards sheet sliding up (≤ 200 ms) and button press states. Everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 9. Home-screen icon

An ink-navy square with the trip drawn as a three-station line: Osaka pink → Kyoto matcha → Tokyo lavender, rising left to right. There's no text in the icon; the home-screen label is "Brillo". It needs 180 (apple-touch-icon), 192, 512 and maskable PNGs (HANDOFF §7). These get exported from the SVG in Phase 1 after approval.
